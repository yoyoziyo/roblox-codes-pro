Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information may be processed when you use 67Codes and the choices available to you.

Effective date: August 16, 2026

1. About 67Codes

67Codes is a static informational website that helps visitors find codes and redemption instructions for Roblox experiences. We do not offer user accounts, payment forms, comments, or a database for personal profiles.

2. Information handled by the site

67Codes stores only your selected language in your browser's local storage so the website can reopen in the language you chose. This preference stays on your device and can be removed through your browser settings.

Our hosting and advertising providers may automatically receive technical information needed to deliver, secure, and measure their services, such as your IP address, browser or device details, page URL, approximate location, access time, and cookie or advertising identifiers.

3. Advertising, cookies, and Google AdSense

We use Google AdSense to display advertisements. Google and its advertising partners may use cookies or similar technologies to deliver and measure ads, prevent fraud, and, where permitted by your choices and local law, personalize advertising.

Visitors in regions where consent is required may be shown a Google-certified consent message. You can accept, decline, or manage the available purposes. You may also control Google ad personalization in Google Ad Settings.

For more detail, read how Google uses information from sites that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

4. Third-party links

67Codes links to Roblox, Discord, and other external websites. When you follow those links, the destination service processes information under its own privacy policy and terms. We do not control their content or privacy practices.

5. Data retention and security

The language preference remains in your browser until you remove it. Retention of technical and advertising information is determined by the relevant service provider. We take reasonable care when selecting and configuring services, but no online transmission or storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

6. Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, privacy law may give you rights regarding access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, or withdrawal of consent. You can manage cookies through your browser, adjust Google ad settings, and use the consent controls displayed on the site where available.

7. Children

67Codes does not knowingly request personal information from children through accounts or submission forms. Parents and guardians should supervise children's internet use and review the privacy settings of Roblox, Discord, Google, and other services they use.

8. Changes and contact

We may update this policy when the website, providers, or legal requirements change. The effective date above shows the latest revision. For privacy questions, contact the 67Codes team through our Discord community.

This page describes the current operation of 67Codes and is intended to provide clear, practical information. It is not legal advice.
