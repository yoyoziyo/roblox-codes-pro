Contact and Corrections

Found an expired code, a missing code, or an incorrect instruction? Send the details to our editorial team.

Last reviewed: October 3, 2026

What you can report

You can report expired or missing codes, incorrect redemption steps, broken links, wrong game information, image problems, and other factual errors.

What to include

Include the game name, the page URL, the code or information involved, and a short explanation. If possible, mention where you found the corrected information.

Editorial contact

Email: privacy@67codes.com. Until a dedicated editorial address is available, this inbox also receives correction reports. We do not provide support for Roblox accounts, purchases, bans, or problems inside individual games.

Correction process

We review the report, compare it with reliable sources, update the page when the correction is confirmed, and record a new review date for meaningful changes.
