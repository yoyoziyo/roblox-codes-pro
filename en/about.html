About us

About 67Codes

67Codes is an independent publication that helps Roblox players find codes, understand how to redeem them, and access practical information about the games they play.

Last reviewed: October 3, 2026

Our purpose

We created 67Codes to make code information easier to find and use. Each page is organized around a specific Roblox experience and is reviewed when we receive or discover relevant changes.

What we publish

We publish active-code lists, redemption instructions, gameplay tips, and, as our editorial library grows, original guides about progression, systems, items, and strategies.

How we work

Our team compares reliable public sources, checks official game channels when available, preserves codes exactly as published, and records the date of each meaningful review. We do not create or guess codes.

Independence

67Codes is not affiliated with Roblox Corporation or with the developers of the experiences listed on this website. Roblox and game names belong to their respective owners.

Help us improve

If you find an expired code or incorrect instruction, contact our editorial team. Reports help us correct pages faster and keep the site useful.
