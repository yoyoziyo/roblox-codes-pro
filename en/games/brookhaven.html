Brookhaven beginner guide

Brookhaven is an open roleplaying experience rather than a game with a fixed campaign. This guide shows how to choose a role, use the city's tools, and build clearer stories with other players.

Brookhaven does not currently provide a promotional code system. This guide focuses on the gameplay information that is useful right now.

Progression

  • Start by choosing a simple role and a location that supports the story you want to play.
  • Expand the scenario gradually with a home, vehicle, job, and other characters.
  • Try different parts of the city to discover settings that fit new roleplay ideas.

Core mechanics

  • Homes and vehicles help establish where your character lives and how they move around the city.
  • Jobs, outfits, emotes, and props communicate your role to other players.
  • Roleplay works best when actions are clear and everyone has room to participate.

Resources and planning

  • Use the vehicle menu when a scene moves to another part of the map.
  • Save outfit and character ideas that can be reused in future stories.
  • Agree on the basic scenario with friends before adding conflicts or surprises.
