Editorial Policy

This policy describes the standards used by the 67Codes editorial team to produce and maintain code pages and game guides.

Last reviewed: October 3, 2026

Research and sources

We use official game pages, developer announcements, update logs, community channels, and established gaming publications. External information is rewritten and organized for our readers; we do not republish complete articles from other websites.

Code status

Active means a code is currently presented as redeemable by the sources reviewed. No active codes means the game has a redemption feature but no confirmed code at the time of review. No code system means we found no confirmed redemption feature in the experience.

Verification

A reviewed code is not necessarily tested inside the game. We only describe a code as tested when a member of our team has redeemed it directly. Codes are preserved with their original capitalization, spacing, numbers, and symbols.

Updates and dates

The review date changes only after a meaningful check or content update. Pages may be reviewed more often when a game receives an update or new codes are announced.

Corrections

When we confirm an error, we correct the page and update its review date. Readers can report expired codes, missing codes, incorrect instructions, or other factual problems through our contact page.

Authors and responsibility

Every editorial page identifies the team member or editorial group responsible for it. Future contributors will receive public profiles listing their role, biography, specialties, and published work.
